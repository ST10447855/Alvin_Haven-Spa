Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, memberships, and booking process below.

What services do you offer?

We provide therapeutic massage, chiropractic care, recovery and mobility treatments, and relaxation and wellness sessions. Visit our Services page for full details and pricing.

Do I need a membership to book?

No, memberships are optional. You can book individual sessions at any time. However, memberships offer significant savings and priority booking benefits.

How do I book a session?

Visit our Enquiry page and fill in the form with your preferred service, date, and time. Our team will confirm your booking within 24 hours.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes. We require at least 24 hours notice for rescheduling or cancellation. Late cancellations may incur a 50% fee. Please contact us via phone or email to make changes.

Where are you located?

Alvin's Haven is located at 45 Wellness Drive, Lynnwood, Pretoria, 0081. We also have a satellite branch at 12 Recovery Lane, Brooklyn, Pretoria, 0181. Visit our Contact page for directions and a map.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring a valid ID, comfortable clothing, and any relevant medical records or referrals. Arrive 10 minutes early to complete a brief wellness assessment form.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, card (Visa/Mastercard), EFT, and SnapScan. Membership fees are collected via monthly debit order.
