OUR STORY

A Haven Built From a Legacy of Care

Alvin's Haven is named in honour of my mother, a dedicated nurse who has spent 21 years caring for others.

Her compassion, patience and commitment inspired the idea behind Alvin's Haven: a welcoming place where people can access quality wellness and recovery support at an affordable price.

We opened in 2021 with a simple goal — to make people feel cared for, especially those looking for support with back pain, muscle tension, mobility and everyday wellbeing.

A legacy of care. A future of wellness. ♡
